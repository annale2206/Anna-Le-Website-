a completely different genealogy of the word "persona" — as a design tool, not an identity theory

Alan Cooper (1999 / 2004)
The Inmates Are Running the Asylum — designers should build fictional personas representing users, prioritizing goals over technology.
Pruitt & Adlin
The Persona Lifecycle — personas as evidence-based fictional users moving through planning, maturation, and eventual retirement inside an organization.
Jung, Salminen, Jansen et al. (2018)
Automatic Persona Generation — personas generated computationally from millions of social media interactions via clustering, no interviews required.
