the direct theoretical center of this research

Marshall & Barbour (2015)
Making Intellectual Room for Persona Studies — persona as a strategic public identity produced through communication, not an authentic inner self.
Marshall, Barbour & Moore (2017)
Five Dimensions of Online Persona — public, mediatised, performative, collective, intentional value. The most-used analytical model in the field.
Moore & Connell (2025)
Podcast persona — extends persona beyond humans toward media objects constructed through audiences, platforms, and producers.
Katja Lee (2019)
Celebrity profiling as life writing — the journalist's persona and the subject's persona are co-produced simultaneously.
