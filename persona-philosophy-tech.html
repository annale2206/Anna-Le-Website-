where persona becomes data — the throughline this dissertation argues from

Roger Clarke (2014)
The digital persona as a data-based proxy: "a model of an individual's public personality based on data and maintained by transactions."
John Cheney-Lippold (2017)
We Are Data — algorithms don't discover identity, they produce it. Introduces "measurable types" and "as if" subjectivity: platforms treat you as if you belong to a category, whether or not it's true.
Yuk Hui (2016)
On the Existence of Digital Objects — profiles, databases, and digital identities as ontological objects composed of data, metadata, and schema.
